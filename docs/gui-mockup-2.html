ARMLOCKEDTick the confirmation. Needs preflight GO and the pilot's switch off Offboard.
TAKEOFFFUTURE, STAGE BNot available: the computer's offboard stream is level attitude at zero thrust, so it cannot fly. The pilot flies in Altitude mode.
LANDFUTURE, STAGE BSame limit. Today the guard's LAND is a failsafe, not a button.
DISARMON GROUND ONLYSends a disarm. After a pilot takeover it is withheld: the pilot's arm switch (ch6) or kill does it.
ABORT MISSIONALWAYS ONOut-of-band stop to every aircraft (takeover.py), works if this window hangs. Never cuts motors in the air.
Kill stays on the pilot's handset (right bumper). It is deliberately not a button here: it drops the aircraft.
